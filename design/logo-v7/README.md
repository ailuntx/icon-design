# Icon Design logo exploration

Three concepts were generated on 2026-09-24 through the user-authorized OpenRouter image endpoint with `bytedance-seed/seedream-5-0-pro`, `1K`, square output, and the prior released logo as a style reference only. Exact prompts and safe receipts accompany the source images. The provider returned 1024 × 1024 JPEG files and reported $0.045 per image, $0.135 total.

`comparison.png` shows the two strongest candidates at large, 64, 32, and 24 pixel sizes. The third candidate was excluded from the review because its puzzle-piece shape was too generic. The user selected B, Color Sail. `selected-logo.png` and `selected-icon.png` are direct 512 and 128 pixel downsamples of its full-square source, and are copied into the plugin assets without changing the composition.
